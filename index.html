How FlowGrove works

One narrow, auditable workflow

📄

1. Supply an authorized file

You provide an Excel or CSV extract, the required columns and relevant naming rules. Redact personal or sensitive information.

🔎

2. Review issues first

We look for missing fields, inconsistent conventions and likely duplicate identifiers. Uncertain choices go to you.

✅

3. Receive an audit trail

You get a review workbook, issues report and change log. Only authorized edits are included; no changes to a live storefront.
